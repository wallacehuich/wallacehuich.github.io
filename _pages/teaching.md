### From Electrons to Igloos: The Molecular Foundations of Arctic Ingenuity (Hong Kong Space Museum, 30 Aug 2025)

I recently gave a public talk at the Hong Kong Space Museum, exploring how molecular arrangements of H₂O underpin the remarkable thermal properties of snow and ice that make igloos viable in extreme cold. We touched on water’s phase diagram, density anomalies, thermal conductivity, and how pore-scale structure and surface effects translate into real-world insulation.
